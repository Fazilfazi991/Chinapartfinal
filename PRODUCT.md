# China Parts Shop product truth

The 7 October 2026 client feedback is authoritative. This is an enquiry-first automotive and equipment parts sourcing service. Visitors describe a requirement, provide a part number or equipment details, and send one enquiry. Availability, fitment, price and delivery require human confirmation. There is no public inventory or purchasing promise.

Primary audiences: workshops and garages, fleet operations, procurement. Capabilities include China-manufactured parts for vehicles and equipment of other origins. Existing categories and brands are review content awaiting final client master lists.

Primary actions: Find Your Part and Send Your Enquiry. WhatsApp Sales uses +44 7520 688566, explicitly approved for client review. Technical assistance enters the same enquiry with technical-help context. One floating WhatsApp control is permitted. The chatbot is deferred. The owner chose direct implementation from the brief, preserving CPS branding.

Supplier registration is a separate private workflow with a persistent business reference and authorized staff review. A vendor reference is neither an account nor a credential. Customers use approved Supabase Auth accounts with company isolation; public registration and reference-only access are unavailable.

English is authoritative. Arabic and Chinese have architecture placeholders only. Public submissions and uploads remain gated until approved anti-abuse and scanner integrations are configured and accepted. Local testing uses synthetic data and explicit development gates. Production deployment, real customer changes, paid services and outgoing messages are outside scope.
